About

Dr. Qingxia Li is Professor of Mathematics and Chair of the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science at Fisk University, where he provides leadership in academic programs, faculty development, curriculum innovation, and student success initiatives. He is committed to expanding access to high-quality STEM education and preparing students for careers in mathematics, computer science, data science, and artificial intelligence.

Dr. Li earned his Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics from Louisiana State University. His research spans applied mathematics, computational science, artificial intelligence in STEM education, and evidence-based teaching and learning. His current work focuses on developing innovative instructional models that integrate data science, AI, and project-based learning into undergraduate STEM curricula to enhance student engagement, persistence, and workforce readiness.

In addition to serving as Chair of the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science, Dr. Li serves as Associate Provost for Research at Fisk University, where he supports institutional research development, faculty scholarship, and interdisciplinary collaboration. He also serves as Director of the Quality Enhancement Plan.

As Principal Investigator and Co-Principal Investigator on multiple federally funded projects, Dr. Li has led initiatives supported by the National Science Foundation (NSF), the Howard Hughes Medical Institute (HHMI), the Mathematical Association of America (MAA), the United Negro College Fund (UNCF), the National Institutes of Health (NIH), the Alfred P. Sloan Foundation, and the U.S. Department of Education.
