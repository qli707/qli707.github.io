About

I am a Professor and Chair in the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science at Fisk University. My research interests lie at the intersection of applied mathematics, computational science, data science, and mathematical modeling.

My research focuses on developing computational and mathematical approaches to address interdisciplinary problems in STEM, with applications in computational biology, biostatistics, data science, and mathematical modeling.

I am also interested in STEM education, undergraduate research, and the development of computational thinking and data-driven skills among undergraduate students.
